MY INTRODUCTION

Interested in Building Intelligent Technology.

I am Paul Akinade Omowonuola, a Robotics and Hardware Developer with a strong interest in the technologies that bring software intelligence and physical systems together.

My interests include robotics, hardware development, artificial intelligence, automation, robotics software, cybersecurity and nanorobotics.

I am particularly interested in software that can support, control and improve robotic and hardware systems, as well as the security challenges that come with connected and intelligent technologies.

I see cybersecurity as an important part of technology development. Intelligent systems should not only be capable; they should also be designed with security and responsible use in mind.

Founder of RHESTAID

RHESTAID is an emerging engineering technology company taking a software-first approach, with longer-term ambitions in AI, robotics, hardware systems, automation and cybersecurity.
