WHO WE ARE

Technology built around ideas and possibilities.

RHESTAID is an emerging engineering technology company founded with an interest in connecting software, engineering and intelligence.

The company's foundation is software development. Its current development work relates to properties and addresses, while its wider ambitions extend into artificial intelligence, robotics, hardware systems and cybersecurity.

RHESTAID aims to develop useful technological solutions while building the knowledge and software foundations for more advanced engineering systems.
