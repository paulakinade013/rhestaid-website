THE FUTURE WE ARE WORKING TOWARDS

Our Vision

A future where intelligent software, advanced engineering and secure technology work together to expand what humans can create.

OUR LONG-TERM DIRECTION

Two Ambitions. One Intelligent Future.

RHESTAID's long-term vision brings together two connected directions: developing AI that helps people create intelligent machines, and developing AI that can eventually operate within those machines.

◇

VISION ONE

AI That Helps Humans Build Robots

We envision developing intelligent software tools that assist people in designing, developing, programming and improving robots.

These tools could help make complex engineering and robotics development more accessible, efficient and intelligent.

◆

VISION TWO

AI That Becomes Part of Intelligent Machines

We also envision AI becoming part of the intelligence systems inside robots and other hardware that RHESTAID may develop in the future.

The ambition is to create machines that can interpret information, make intelligent decisions and respond meaningfully to their environments.

OUR APPROACH

Building From Software First

RHESTAID is taking a software-first approach. Our present development focus is software, including solutions relating to properties and addresses.

This provides a foundation for developing practical digital systems while building knowledge and capabilities that can support our wider ambitions in artificial intelligence, robotics and hardware.

Robotics hardware development is a future ambition, not a claim about what the company currently produces.

◇

From Software to Intelligent Systems

Our intended journey is to grow through software development, explore increasingly capable AI systems, and eventually connect intelligent software with physical machines and hardware.

A FOUNDATION FOR THE FUTURE

Intelligence Must Be Secure

As software and intelligent devices become more connected, security must be considered from the beginning—not added as an afterthought.

◇

Intelligence

Exploring AI systems that can support people, processes and future intelligent machines.

◆

Engineering

Applying software and engineering thinking to practical problems and future technologies.

⬡

Security

Building with cybersecurity awareness and protecting connected systems, information and technology.

THE RHESTAID AMBITION

Engineering Intelligence. Securing the Future.

We are building towards a future where software, artificial intelligence, robotics and engineering come together to create meaningful possibilities.
